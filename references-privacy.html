Nico YT Studio References

Nico YT Studio References is a private, internal tool of Nico YT Studio, a small independent studio that makes educational animated videos for YouTube. Its only user is the studio owner. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

What data we access

The studio owner connects his own Pinterest account with read-only access. The tool then reads the boards of this account and the Pins saved on them (title, link, image or video of each saved Pin), through the official Pinterest API. It does not access the Pins, boards or personal information of any other Pinterest user.

How we use it

The saved Pins are motion-design and illustration references curated by the studio owner. We look at how their animations are paced and write our own notes, in our own words (timings, colours, gestures). These notes guide the art direction of our own videos.

Storage and retention

Pinterest data is only displayed during the working session. When a video Pin is studied, its video is processed in a temporary folder on one computer and deleted right after the analysis. We do not keep Pinterest data: we keep only our own written notes.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, publish or transfer Pinterest data to anyone. We do not redistribute Pins, we never publish anything to Pinterest, and we do not use Pinterest content to train any artificial intelligence model.

Your choices

Access granted to the tool can be revoked at any time from the connected apps section of the Pinterest account settings. Any question or request about this policy can be sent to the contact address below; we answer within 7 days.

Cookies and advertising

The tool uses no cookies and shows no advertising.
