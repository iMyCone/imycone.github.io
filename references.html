Nico YT Studio References

Nico YT Studio References is the internal reference tool of Nico YT Studio, a small independent studio that makes educational animated videos for YouTube.

The studio owner connects his own Pinterest account, read-only, so the tool can show him the motion-design and illustration references saved on his own boards. We study how these animations are paced and write our own notes to guide the art direction of our videos. The tool is not offered to the public, publishes nothing and keeps no Pinterest data.
